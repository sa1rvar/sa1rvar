I'm Sarvar, an independent researcher interested in the economies of countries — how they grow, struggle, and change over time. Currently, my focus is on Uzbekistan's economy: how it has developed, and particularly its trade relationships and economic ties with other countries.


**Background:**
Before moving into this area, I studied backend development, which gave me a strong foundation in working with data and building systems — skills I now bring into analyzing economic data and trends. Alongside that, I've spent time learning languages, having reached fluency in Russian and English, which lets me draw on sources and research across both regional and international perspectives.




I'm especially interested in questions like: how has Uzbekistan's trade with its neighbors and major partners evolved, what drives these relationships, and what they mean for the country's economic path forward.

I'm always open to connecting with others interested in economics, trade, or Central Asian development — feel free to reach out.
